The WGSL implementation ports the default ICtCp branch and published defaults.
CPU curve initialization follows the original C++ methods. Workbench adapters
convert AP0/D60 input to Rec.2020/D65 and Rec.2020 output to linear Rec.709,
and express returned luminance relative to the source's SDR paper white.
The original source and presentation downloads remain in an ignored cache.

See references/entries/gt7.md for complete source provenance, unit conversion,
HDR domain, fidelity and validation details.
